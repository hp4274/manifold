Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

Manifold Clean Energy Pvt. Ltd. ("Manifold", "we", "us") collects personal information so that we can answer enquiries, assess product applications, deliver and service our products, and meet our legal obligations. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email address, phone numbers, city and postal address.
  • Application details: identification numbers and documents, property or vehicle details, fuel and water supply information, usage estimates and payment preferences.
  • Correspondence: the content of emails, calls and enquiry forms.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected in ordinary server logs.

How we use it

  • To respond to an enquiry or process an application.
  • To carry out verification, technical assessment and site inspection where a product is involved.
  • To deliver, install, service and warrant products.
  • To send service notices, and marketing only where you have asked for it.
  • To comply with tax, safety and regulatory requirements.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with parties who need it to deliver what you asked for: authorised installation and service partners, logistics providers, and financing institutions where you have applied for financing. We also disclose information where the law or a regulator requires it. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for two years. Application and installation records are kept for the life of the product plus the statutory retention period that applies to warranty and tax records. You can ask us to delete data sooner — see the Data Deletion policy.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Write to info@manifoldcleanenergy.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Uploaded documents and application records are stored with access limited to the review team. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Deletion

Last updated 6 August 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time.

How to ask

  • Email info@manifoldcleanenergy.com with the subject line "Data deletion request".
  • Include the name, email address and phone number you used when contacting us, so we can find the records.
  • We may ask one verification question before acting, to make sure the request is genuine.

What happens next

We acknowledge the request within 3 working days and complete it within 30 days. Uploaded identity and address documents are deleted first. You receive written confirmation when the deletion is done.

What we may keep

We keep the minimum records that the law requires us to retain — invoices and tax records, safety and installation records for an installed product, and any records needed to defend a legal claim. These are kept in restricted storage and are not used for any other purpose.

Once records are deleted we cannot process a pending application, honour a warranty claim that relies on the deleted installation record, or restore the data later.

Cookie Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. This site keeps its use of them to a minimum.

What this site uses

  • Strictly necessary: session cookies that keep a form working while you fill it in. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preference: a cookie remembering choices you have made, such as a dismissed notice.

What this site does not use

We do not run advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels or profiling tools on this site.

Controlling cookies

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop forms on this site from working correctly.
