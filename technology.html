Kinetic7 Hydrogen-On-Demand™

We do not store hydrogen.
We make it at the burner.

Water and electricity go in, a clean flame comes out, and nothing sits in a cylinder in between. No pipeline to dig, no refuelling station to reach, no fuel in the building when the kitchen is empty.

How it works

Three steps, and none of them involve a delivery.

An electrolytic cell splits water into hydrogen and oxygen and sends the gas straight to the burner. Generation starts when you open the dial and stops when you close it.

  1. 01

    Water goes in

    Purified water fills the tank. That is the only fuel the system consumes — roughly 60 ml for every kilowatt-hour of cooking. The electrolyte itself recirculates, so only the water actually turned into gas ever leaves the loop.

  2. 02

    Electricity splits it

    The cell separates the water into hydrogen and oxygen — HHO gas. It is produced at the rate the burner is using it, which is why there is no tank of fuel anywhere in the appliance and nothing to refill.

  3. 03

    The flame does the rest

    The gas is conditioned, dried and burned. The only combustion product is water vapour: no carbon dioxide at the point of use, no smoke, no soot on the base of the pan.

Safety by design

The safest gas is the one that was never stored.

Gas exists only while the appliance is running, and only between the cell and the burner. Everything below sits in that short path.

  • 01

    Bubbler

    A water-sealed trap takes any liquid carried along with the gas back out of the stream.

  • 02

    Demister

    A coalescing filter catches electrolyte aerosol and returns it to the tank.

  • 03

    Desiccant stage

    Dries the gas below 5% moisture, which is what gives the flame its stability.

  • 04

    Dual flashback arrestors

    Certified sintered stainless elements on both gas lines stop a flame travelling back into the unit.

  • 05

    Continuous monitoring

    Temperature, voltage, current, gas flow, humidity, conductivity and tank level all feed automatic shut-off interlocks, with the readings logged.

Every flame counted

Usage data, not estimates.

The stoves meter themselves. Usage streams from the kitchen to a registry, which turns clean cooking into audit-grade evidence rather than a survey answer — the basis for verified carbon credits under standards such as Verra and Gold Standard.

The same readings tell an owner what their cooking actually costs, in water and in electricity, day by day.
