This technology is powered by K7 Technology

The cell at the heart of every Manifold product is K7 Technology's: water is split at the moment of use and burned within the second, so there is nothing stored, nothing pressurised and nothing to transport. The cooking stove and the TukTuk conversion kit run on the same engine.
