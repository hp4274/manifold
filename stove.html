Overview

A stove that makes its own fuel, one flame at a time.

The Kinetic stove replaces the cylinder with a compact electrolysis cell. Purified water and a household power socket are all it needs. Hydrogen is produced only while a burner is lit, so there is nothing stored between meals and nothing to deliver, book or queue for.

The flame behaves the way cooks expect — instant ignition, a wide throttle range and enough heat for a full tadka — while the only exhaust is water vapour.

  • Zero smoke, zero soot, zero CO2 at the burner
  • Works with every standard pan, kadai and pressure cooker
  • No cylinder booking, no refill queue, no delivery charge
  • Runs on 230 V household power and purified water

How it works

Water in, flame out, water vapour back.

Four steps, all of them happening inside the stove while a burner is lit.

  1. 01

    Fill the tank

    Pour 1.6 litres of purified water into the front-loading tank. A float gauge shows the level.

  2. 02

    Turn the dial

    The cell starts generating hydrogen the moment a burner is opened, and stops when it closes.

  3. 03

    Cook as usual

    Gas reaches the burner at a steady low pressure. Ignition, throttle and simmer feel familiar.

  4. 04

    Vapour out

    Combustion returns the hydrogen to water vapour. Nothing else leaves the burner.

Questions

What buyers ask before the first cook.

Anything not covered here, write to us and an engineer will answer.

Is it safe to make hydrogen in a kitchen?

The stove never holds a reserve. Hydrogen is generated at low pressure only while a burner is open, and the volume inside the appliance at any moment is smaller than the gas in a lit cigarette lighter. Four independent cut-offs close the cell if a flame goes out, the tank runs dry, pressure rises or the unit overheats.

What happens during a power cut?

The cell stops and the flame goes out safely. The KH-100 can be paired with any 1 kVA inverter for uninterrupted cooking; an integrated battery option is planned for the next revision.

Can I use ordinary tap water?

No. Dissolved salts foul the cell membrane. Use RO or distilled water below 20 ppm TDS — the supplied jug has an inline filter for everyday top-ups.

How much does it cost to run?

Roughly ₹18 of electricity a day for a family of four cooking three meals, based on a tariff of ₹8 per unit. Water use is about 1.5 litres a day.

Does the flame work with my existing pans?

Yes. The burner spacing and pan support match a standard two-burner hob, so kadai, tawa, pressure cooker and flat-bottom pans all sit correctly.
